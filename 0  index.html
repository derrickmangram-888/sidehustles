    • *Research and content creation

    • *Customer service and support

    • Potential earnings:

  •  Duties include:

    • *Conducting virtual classes and sessions

    • *Assigning and grading homework

    • *Providing feedback and support



    • Initial investment:


    • $0 - $100 (depending on software and equipment)



    • Potential earnings:


    • $20 - $50 per hour

  • As a freelance writer, you'll create content for clients, including blog posts, articles, and website copy.


  •  Duties include:


    • *Researching and writing high-quality content

    • *Editing and proofreading

    • *Meeting deadlines and client requirements


    • Initial investment: 


    • $0 - $100 (depending on software and equipment)


    • Potential earnings: 


    • $25 - $100 per article

  • ## SH #7:


  • Pet Sitting and Dog Walking ##


  •  If you love animals, you can offer pet sitting and dog walking services in your local area.


  • Duties include:


    • *Caring for pets in their own homes

    • *Taking dogs for walks and providing exercise

    • *Administering medication and providing basic care



    • Initial investment:


    • $0 - $100 (depending on equipment and marketing)



    • Potential earnings:


    • $15 - $30 per hour



  • ## SH #8: House Sitting ##

    •


  •  Similar to pet sitting, you can offer house sitting services to homeowners who need someone to watch their property while they're away.


  •  Duties include:


    • *Watering plants and collecting mail

    • *Keeping an eye on the property and reporting any issues

    • *Providing basic maintenance and upkeep



    • Initial investment:


    • $0 - $100 (depending on equipment and marketing)


    • Potential earnings:

  • Uber Eats, DoorDash, or GrubHub.


  • Duties include:


    • *Picking up and delivering food and packages

    • *Providing excellent customer service

    • *Managing your schedule and routes



    • Initial investment:


    •  $0 - $100 (depending on equipment and vehicle)


    • Potential earnings:


    • $10 - $25 per hour

    • Initial investment:
